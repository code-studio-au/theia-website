Stoneford grew around a river crossing in the Upper Heartlands. Farm carts come up from Millhaven, caravans stop to unload, and the roads carry travellers farther inland toward Valecrown. The river drives the mills as well as shaping the roads. It’s the reason so much of the town’s work happens within sight of the bridge.

## The people who keep Stoneford moving

Brenna Holt’s trade links Millhaven with Stoneford’s warehouses. Her work brings her into contact with teamsters, storekeepers, and the people who know which cargo arrived on time. Couriers carry dockets between the yard and the town, while the reeve has to balance the needs of local traffic with those of passing caravans. When the bridge needs repairs, that becomes a problem every household can feel.

## Around the market and beyond

The zone’s design brings the market, inn, workshops, and caravan yards together around the crossing. The market is where travellers replenish food and supplies, and the workshops provide a place to repair equipment before the next leg of the road. A separate work track leads towards Greyfall Quarry. There, Joss has a more immediate concern than the next caravan. Some quarry workers are missing, and the old shift tally is a place to start looking.

Stoneford is part of the Human campaign, and its local stories give it a life beyond the main investigation. Checking on a missing delivery, helping reopen a bridge, and finding out who made it home from a shift all matter to the people who live here.
