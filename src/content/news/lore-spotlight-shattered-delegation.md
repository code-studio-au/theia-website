Before Theia’s story begins, Covenant and Dominion sent delegations to discuss peace. Even getting representatives of the two alliances to agree to talks was a rare achievement. It took people willing to defend the talks at home, where any concession could be held against them.

## When the talks fell silent

An attack devastated the gathering. Among the dead was Lord Edric Halewyn, the first royal counsellor and trusted adviser to King Aldren. Edric had helped make the talks possible. The loss reached the Dominion leadership just as personally, taking someone close to one of its orc leaders.

The surviving representatives brought home conflicting accounts. Witnesses remembered different things, and the documents recovered after the attack appeared to support accusations on both sides. Covenant blamed Dominion. Dominion blamed Covenant. The talks ended, and calls for retaliation drowned out the voices that had argued for peace.

## An event that still shapes the war

Coastal garrisons reinforced their defences and expeditions pushed into contested territory. People who had backed the negotiations now had to answer for their judgement. For families who lost someone, the delegation remained a recent wound long after officials turned it into an argument for continuing the war.

The Shattered Delegation matters to the present-day story because its two public histories are still in circulation. As you meet the people who inherited them, pay attention to who can tell you what they saw and who can only tell you what they were told.
