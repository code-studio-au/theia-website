We’re planning a suggestions board where you can share feature requests and ideas for Theia, read what others have posted, and upvote the suggestions you’d most like us to explore. Ideas with more votes will rise in the list, so it’s easier to see where the community shares an interest.

## What to suggest

You might have an idea for finding a group, a clearer way to track a quest, or an accessibility setting that would make the game easier to play. Give your idea a short title and describe when it would help. If someone has already suggested something similar, you can add your support by voting for it.

## How we’ll use the votes

The ranking will help us see which ideas players care about most. We’ll consider that alongside playtest feedback, the work involved, and how a suggestion fits into Theia. We’ll share where to find the board when it opens.
