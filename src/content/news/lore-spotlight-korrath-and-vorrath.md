Theia’s trolls trace their beginnings to the Keeper and the Ascendant. Their oldest homeland lay in rugged highlands, where traditions honouring both gods took root. The two peoples known today as the Korrath and the Vorrath grew out of that shared history.

## Korrath: the old highland home

The ancestral highland capital survived the Divine War. The Korrath retained it and formed a tradition centred on the Keeper, with councils of elders responsible for preserving chronicles and passing down protective practices. The city’s survival gave generations of Korrath a place to return to and records against which to test their own accounts of the past.

## Vorrath: exile and the storm coast

The Vorrath carry forward the older Ascendant-linked tradition. Religious differences became political disputes over leadership, custom, and sacred places. The conflict drove the people who became the Vorrath into exile. They established a new sacred capital on the storm coast, led by religious authorities. Pilgrimage and the coast’s difficult weather became part of a life built far from the highland home.

## The alliances came later

The Korrath eventually joined Covenant and the Vorrath joined Dominion, long after the religious split. Their histories still disagree over the circumstances of the exile. An old chronicle preserved in the highlands and a tradition carried to the coast can tell different parts of the same migration.
