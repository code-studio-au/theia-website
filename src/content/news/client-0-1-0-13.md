Client **0.1.0.13** is available through the Linux launcher on the Development channel.

## Millhaven and the Sydney realm

This build includes the seven-zone Millhaven test world. Explore its terrain and village landmarks, try the modular character appearances, and look for the animated wolf during play. Invited testers can enter the Sydney development realm with the realm configuration supplied for the test.

## Characters and saved progress

Sign in with the same tester account to return to your characters. The realm stores character progress between sessions, including supported inventory and quest progress. During a playtest, try signing out and back in to check that your latest progress is present.

## Linux download

The optimized client ships with the files needed to play. This release trims debug symbols and development-only asset files to reduce the initial download.
