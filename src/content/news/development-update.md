Theia Online is in active development. Our latest work brings the journey into the world closer together, from launching the client to finding your way around Stoneford.

## A new beginning

The standalone Linux launcher can install and update the game from trusted local release packages. It verifies installed files and can restore a previous release. Its harbour artwork and gold framing introduce the visual language carried through the game interface.

Public hosted downloads and a Windows installer are still in development. We will publish availability here when they are ready.

## Finding your way

The in-game interface has received a new pass: rounded character frames, portrait choices, a framed minimap and world map, a gold skill dock and a violet experience bar. Recent chat work adds speech bubbles to nearby conversations and improves the way longer messages fit in the history panel.

## The journey ahead

We are building and testing Theia in small playable steps. Development updates describe the work completed so far; the wider world and campaign remain in development.
