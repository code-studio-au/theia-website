Across Theia, the remains of an extraordinary age stand beside the lives of ordinary people. Broken passageways, forgotten archives and unfamiliar mechanisms hint at knowledge the modern world no longer fully understands.

Among these mysteries are the Anchors.

## Echoes of an ancient ambition

The first of these structures belongs to a history scarred by catastrophe. Its legacy reaches far beyond a single ruin. Yet the mechanisms encountered in the modern world are not all the same: later Anchors are imperfect echoes of an older design.

That distinction matters. A small resonator can disturb its immediate surroundings. More substantial installations draw on supporting structures and recovered artifacts. Their appearance may be local, but their purpose invites much larger questions.

## More than a ruin

Who is rebuilding these devices? Which fragments of knowledge survived, and who has chosen to keep them hidden? The answers are woven into Theia's story, to be uncovered through witnesses, evidence and the places they have left behind.

Not every old monument is an Anchor. Not every account of the past tells the same story. The truth is something you will have to seek.
