These notes cover Linux client **0.1.0.11** on the Development channel.

## Settings and display modes

- The Interface, Support, and Developer panels now share the styling introduced in Graphics.
- Choose Windowed, Borderless, or Fullscreen in Graphics. After a display change, select **Keep Changes** to confirm it. If the confirmation expires, the client restores the previous mode.

## Known issue

The Interface settings panel can overlap combat feedback.

## Availability

This build is available through the local development release library. Public downloads will be announced on this site.
