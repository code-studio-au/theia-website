# Content sources

Website copy was checked against the local `/home/steven/dev/theia-mmo` checkout on 23 November 2026. No files in that repository were modified.

## Authority and scope

- `docs/story/README.md`: story authority hierarchy; narrative plans are distinct from implemented game content.
- `docs/story/THEIA_WORLD_BIBLE.md`: creative pillars, divine history, Anchors, eight peoples, Covenant/Dominion, martial and magical traditions.
- `docs/story/Theia/Campaigns/CURRENT_HUMAN_CAMPAIGN.md` and `Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md`: current Human campaign. Used for early Heartlands locations; later revelations and endings are deliberately omitted from public copy.
- Individual Atlas place files retain provisional geography labels. The site does not invent a final map, distances or launch region availability.
- `docs/STATUS.md` (23 November entries), `docs/GAME_LAUNCHER.md`: launcher, HUD and chat development update; public hosted delivery and Windows installer are still open.
- `docs/COMBAT_TARGETING_MOVEMENT_SPEC.md` as referenced by the game repository's contributor guidance: classic selected-target combat direction, rather than unsupported action-combat promises.

## Editorial decisions

The homepage follows the visual reference but avoids invented events, public downloads or account services. The third news card is “A World Worth Sharing”, because no scheduled community events were verified. News dates are website editorial dates; only the development update describes dated implementation work.

The racial page includes all eight civilizations, not just the three pictured in the visual reference. The factions are permanently opposed. Crafting is presented as planned. Illustrative artwork is identified on the media page and is not claimed to be gameplay footage.

The public story teases mysteries without publishing creator-facing twists, hidden histories or campaign endings.
