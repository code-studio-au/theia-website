## World page illustrated map

The user supplied `Theia_Post_Sundering_Terrace_Valley_v2 (1).png` as the geography reference. The built-in image generation tool restyled it as a dark-ocean relief map. At the user's direction, the detached top-centre and bottom-centre expansion continents are omitted entirely, and the central land is completely concealed by a swirling dark shroud. The two main continents retain their reference arrangement and terrain identities. This remains illustrative geography rather than a finalized playable map.

The final master is `assets/world-map-shrouded.png` (1254 × 1254); the WebP quality-88 export is `public/images/world-map-shrouded.webp`. Only the final shrouded map is packaged for the website. The World page uses a full-width background with CSS fades at its top and bottom. The description sits directly below the title, and the four story panels overlay the map with dark translucent surfaces for readability. On desktop, the map fills the World section using centred cover sizing: excess top and bottom artwork is cropped on ultrawide displays, while the fades follow the visible section edges. The section clips overflow so artwork cannot enlarge the document or spill below the footer. Mobile keeps the complete map width behind the stacked content.
